ARENA_04 // STRATEGY / CONTROLLED COMBAT

Robo War Rules

Outmaneuver or disable the opponent inside the designated arena using only mechanisms approved during technical inspection.

TECHNICAL INSPECTIONFAIR PLAYSAFETY PROTOCOL

1. Objective

  • Outmaneuver or disable the opposing robot within the designated combat arena.
  • A robot may be defeated if it becomes immobilised, exits the arena or meets another announced defeat condition.

2. Approved Systems

  • Only mechanisms approved during technical inspection may be used.
  • Systems that create unreasonable risk to participants, spectators or venue equipment are prohibited.

3. Arena Conduct

  • Teams must never attack outside the arena.
  • Targeting a person, controller, cable or safety equipment is prohibited.
  • Judges may pause or terminate a match for safety, arena damage or rule violations.

4. Safety

  • All robots must pass technical inspection.
  • Organisers may stop, isolate or remove an unsafe robot at any time.
