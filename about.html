Built for curiosity.

APS LBS RoboKriti 2026 brings robotics, engineering and competitive problem solving into one high-energy student experience.

The mission

Encourage students to build, test, collaborate and solve problems through practical robotics. The championship is designed to make engineering tangible: a machine is not just a finished object, but a sequence of decisions.

The four arenas

Robo Race tests speed and precision. Robo Soccer tests control and teamwork. Robo Tug of War tests traction and power. Robo War tests strategy and controlled combat.

Leadership

Principal Mrs. Sadhana Devi provides institutional leadership, while Mentor Ms. Akansha Rani supports the Tinkering Lab's culture of experimentation and student learning.
