PHASE_01 // ORIGIN

Where ideas become machines.

APS LBS RoboKriti 2026 is built around experimentation, engineering discipline, teamwork and problem solving. The objective is simple: turn a concept into a machine that can perform under pressure.

ENGINEERING LOOP

Prototype → Test → Improve → Compete

Every arena rewards a different engineering mindset. Participants learn by building, debugging, adapting and making decisions when the clock is running.

CHAMPIONSHIP DNA

Speed. Control. Power. Strategy.

From precision racing to controlled combat, RoboKriti turns the Tinkering Lab into an arena for practical innovation.
