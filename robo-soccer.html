ARENA_02 // CONTROL / TEAMWORK

Robo Soccer Rules

Master robotic movement, passing and scoring through controlled tactical gameplay.

TECHNICAL INSPECTIONFAIR PLAYSAFETY PROTOCOL

1. Objective

  • Score more valid goals than the opposing team within the announced match duration.
  • Teams must operate their robots only within the designated playing area.

2. Match Protocol

  • Robots start in their assigned zones and begin on the judge’s signal.
  • Restart procedures, goal confirmation and match timing are controlled by the referee.
  • Deliberate interference outside normal gameplay is prohibited.

3. Robot Control

  • Each team must use the approved control arrangement announced during briefing.
  • Only inspected robots may enter the arena.
  • Teams are responsible for safe operation of their equipment.

4. Fair Play & Safety

  • Unsafe contact, intentional damage or targeting people/equipment is prohibited.
  • Judges may pause or terminate a match for safety or rule violations.
