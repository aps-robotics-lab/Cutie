ARENA_03 // POWER / TRACTION

Robo Tug of War Rules

Use traction, torque and controlled mechanical force to pull the opposing robot across the designated line.

TECHNICAL INSPECTIONFAIR PLAYSAFETY PROTOCOL

1. Objective

  • Pull the opposing robot across the designated line.
  • A clear loss, immobilisation or line crossing determines the result as defined in the briefing.

2. Starting Protocol

  • Robots begin in marked starting zones.
  • The match starts only on the judge’s signal.
  • Teams may not physically touch or manipulate a robot after the round begins unless the judge stops it.

3. Mechanisms

  • Hooks, tethers and pulling mechanisms must meet safety requirements.
  • Mechanisms must not create unreasonable risk to people or the venue.
  • Excessive force or unsafe mechanisms may be stopped by the judge.

4. Safety

  • Every robot must pass technical inspection.
  • Power systems and batteries must remain within announced limits.
